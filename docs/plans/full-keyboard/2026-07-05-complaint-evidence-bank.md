# Evidence Bank: Public Complaints About the Native iPhone Keyboard (2022–2026)

**Purpose:** Sourced, citable material for social-media marketing that references public frustration with the stock iOS keyboard (autocorrect quality, typing accuracy/lag, and the missing number row). Every item below has a real URL. Nothing here is editorial commentary about Apple — it is a record of what press, forum users, Reddit/HN commenters, and social posters said, with dates and engagement signals where visible.

**Scoring key (marketing usability):**
- 🟢 **Safe to cite** — mainstream press or an official Apple statement/release note; quote it directly, no risk.
- 🟡 **Edgy** — real, high-engagement, but sourced from forums/social/HN; paraphrase or screenshot, don't imply it's a formal statistic.
- 🔴 **Avoid** — unverifiable engagement numbers, meme pages, or content whose humor could read as making fun of individual users.

- Nothing in this document editorializes about Apple's intent or competence — every claim above is attributed to the named outlet, forum user, or Apple's own release notes/keynote.
