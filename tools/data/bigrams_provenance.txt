Extraction
----------
- Kept bigrams where BOTH head and continuation exist in the production 50k
  lexicon (Keyboard/Resources/qwerty_lexicon_en.bin), lowercased.
- Continuations ordered by the source's own frequency attribute (f=1 = most
  frequent, per the source's scripts/wordlist.py), capped at top-8 per head
  (the source itself caps at 3, so the cap does not bind).
- Source: 104,703 bigram lines across 47,184 heads. Kept after lexicon
  filtering and dedupe: 27,982 heads, 70,757 bigram entries
  (continuations-per-head distribution — 1: 4847, 2: 3495, 3: 19640).
  Dropped: 30,701 lines with out-of-lexicon heads, 1,021 with out-of-lexicon
  continuations; the remainder of the delta is case-fold dedupe (first
  occurrence wins).
- Packed format: "NPBG" | version UInt8=1 | headCount UInt32 | headCount x
  (headRank UInt16, contCount UInt8, contCount x contRank UInt16), little-
  endian, heads sorted by headRank ascending (see QwertyNextWordPredictor.swift).
