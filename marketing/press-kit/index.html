+ New in 2.0

Live Math Preview

Type “84.50*1.18” in any app and a floating chip shows the answer before you reach “=”. Tap it to insert the result.

Siri & Shortcuts

“Calculate with NumPad,” “Convert units,” and “Calculate a tip” work in Siri and Spotlight the moment the app installs — zero setup, all offline.

Liquid Glass themes

On iOS 26 the Glass theme pair renders Apple’s real Liquid Glass material — refractive, depth-aware, with a matched dark twin.

Custom Keyboard editor

Pro users build their own layout around the keypad: a drag-and-drop key library, live preview, and a left/right-handed toggle.

Two new packs

Units & Conversion and Cooking & Baking join Finance, Symbols & Science, Programmer, and Date & Time — with a live offline converter.

iPad, properly

Side-panel overlays instead of covered work, pointer hover on keys, drag-and-drop rows, and a Kiosk height preset for point-of-sale iPads.

# The privacy story

NumPad’s keyboard extension contains no analytics SDK and never logs a keystroke — not as a policy promise, but as a build decision: Firebase is not linked into the extension binary at all. Full Access, if granted, powers exactly three things — clipboard history, haptics, and key-click sounds — entirely on-device. All math, including the Siri intents, runs offline.
